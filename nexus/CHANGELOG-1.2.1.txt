Diet Dr Camera 1.2.1

Returns to the released 1.2.0 camera and external True Directional Movement
integration while retaining the later bug fixes. The native movement, jumping,
targeting, melee-contact and comparison-recorder experiments are removed.

- Exclude Dismembering Framework blood spray and its invisible surface traces
  from NPC magic flyby noise, preventing cosmetic blood from shaking the camera.
- Prevent already-passed or impacted projectiles from retriggering flyby noise
  after observation gaps, camera stalls, POV changes or listener teleports.
- Controller and arrow navigation follows the visible layout: nearby controls
  and panels in the pressed direction determine the destination. Tabs and
  environment toggles no longer force focus into an unrelated entry list.
- Improve controller and arrow navigation between short entry lists and lower
  editor controls throughout the menu. Returning from a list preserves the
  previous slider row. Tab hints now distinguish Paste Entry to All from
  Paste Entire Tab according to what was copied.
- Paste a copied entry onto a tab to apply it to every compatible entry in
  that tab, including Outdoor and Indoor settings. Use the existing Paste
  binding over the tab header, or right-click and choose Paste Entry to All.
- Add automatic DBVO 2 support to third-person dialogue: frame the player's
  head while choosing a response and keep that shot through native voice
  playback, including delayed or skipped lines. Speaker turns accelerate
  smoothly and preserve the sideways framing while leaving the player shot.
  Existing preset values remain.
- Prevent a quick R3 tap from starting a native camera zoom. First-to-third
  and third-to-first now share the same 0.2-second hold requirement and switch
  on release; TDM target-lock input keeps its existing handling.
- Set the oldest supported Skyrim SE runtime to 1.5.97. Earlier SE patches are
  outside support following the Menu Framework startup failure on 1.5.73.
- Fix startup validation when CBPC installs an inline function-entry detour.
- Preserve native return values from arrow, missile, cone and beam impact callbacks.
- Revalidate complete call sites before installing hooks; retain DDC camera hook chaining.
- Respect foreign collision patches and reject out-of-range collision diversions.
- Restore shock damage reactions using the actual affected actor values and
  retain accepted, collision-bounded shock-beam near-miss effects.
- Silence the camera ambience during recoverable ragdolls.
- Keep target-lock acquisition/release timing consistent across frame rates and
  measure target switches from the remaining visible swing.
- Restore crosshair transforms only after tracing owned them, and release target-lock
  visibility even when projectile tracing is disabled.
- Continue reading format-14 development presets, preserving authored camera
  values while ignoring the retired native vertical-framing field.
- Keep automated build, packaging and symbol checks in the background.

Existing 1.2.0 features and settings remain. Target-lock controls use external
TDM again. Automated validation and remaining gameplay coverage are recorded
in RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md.
