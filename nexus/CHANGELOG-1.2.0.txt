Diet Dr Camera 1.2.0 (changes since 1.1.1)

- Better startup logging for compatibility reports: Skyrim/SKSE versions,
  Address Library details, loaded DLL versions, build identity and precise
  hook-failure information. Keeps three previous DDC logs. No Verbose Logging
  toggle is needed for startup errors.
- Added Damage Reaction with separate first- and third-person intensity.
  Incoming weapons, creature attacks and harmful magic have distinct motion;
  expanded coverage includes spider webs/spit and DLC/Creation Club creatures.
  Dragon and centurion breath keep their dedicated cinematic noise controls.
- NPC arrows, bolts and magic projectiles create stronger cinematic noise as
  they pass closer to the player. Magic has twice the radius and 50% greater
  base intensity than arrows. NPC flybys do not use the player's Repulse tuning.
- Added Crowd Modifier under Cinematic Effects, with separate zoom and FOV
  intensity. It is disabled while paragliding.
- Added Target Lock Height, Zoom and FOV Bias alongside Pitch Bias.
- Projectile Tracing now has independent First Person and Third Person settings.
  Improved fired-spell tracking and contact handling.
- Improved controller hints, clipboard guidance and preset selection/saving.
- Kept first-person cinematic and nearby-enemy noise independent of the
  player's noise profile. Fixed false reactions from non-damaging stat effects.
- Matched Reset button sizing across Projectile Tracing, Death and Ragdoll Camera.
- Updated hook validation for reviewed 1.7 camera/furniture callbacks and made
  conflicting hooks easier to diagnose.

Existing presets remain readable; new Damage Reaction and Target Lock bias
controls default to off. Cinematic Views is not included in this release.
The experimental paraglider timing fixes were reverted; original follow
behavior is retained.

Older-runtime compatibility is still being investigated. If startup fails,
attach the complete DietDrCamera.log and skse64.log from the same launch,
plus the full error message and a crash log if one was generated. Startup
logging works automatically. More detail is in LOGS AND SUPPORT.txt.
