Diet Dr Camera 1.2.0

- Added Damage Reaction for incoming weapon, creature and magic hits, with separate first- and third-person intensity.
- Added arrow, bolt and magic flyby noise. Closer passes feel stronger; magic has more range and intensity.
- Added Crowd Modifier to adjust zoom and FOV around nearby enemies. Disabled while paragliding.
- Added Height, Zoom and FOV Bias to Target Lock.
- Split Projectile Tracing into first- and third-person settings and improved spell tracking.
- Improved preset saving, controller hints and Reset button sizing.
- Fixed unwanted hit reactions and first-person noise conflicts.
- Improved Skyrim 1.7 startup compatibility.
- Improved automatic startup logging and kept three previous logs. Verbose Logging is not required.

Existing presets remain compatible. New Damage Reaction and Target Lock bias controls start disabled. Cinematic Views is not included.

Older-version issues are still being investigated. For startup errors, send DietDrCamera.log and skse64.log from the same launch, plus the full error message.
