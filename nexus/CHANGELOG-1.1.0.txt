Diet Dr Camera 1.1.0

- Fixed a reproduced startup crash on Skyrim 1.5.97 when SKSE Menu Framework
  loads after Diet Dr Camera; improved runtime hook checks and failure details.
- Click a slider value to select its digits in yellow and type a supported
  value. Enter or clicking away applies it; Escape cancels.
- Copy and paste entire configuration tabs from their headers or context menus.
- Added Pitch Bias below Aim Bias in Target Lock transition overrides. Positive
  values pitch up and negative values pitch down as the enemy gets closer.
- Matched all Transition Override toggle sizes and corrected the popup heading.
- Removed the Projectile Tracing Smoothing slider and fixed its behavior at
  the former 0.30 setting.
- Published full versioned source and detailed build instructions on GitHub.

Startup fixes were tested on Steam 1.5.97, 1.6.1170 and 1.7.104. These startup
checks do not establish full gameplay coverage for every supported runtime.
Existing presets remain compatible; Pitch Bias defaults to zero.
