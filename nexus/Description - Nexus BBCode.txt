[size=5][b]Diet Dr Camera[/b][/size]

A configurable camera overhaul for Skyrim with per-state framing, smooth transitions,
first-person FOV and effects, dialogue and menu cameras, and shareable presets.
Configure the camera in game through SKSE Menu Framework and Quick Tune.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Third-person camera profiles for movement, combat, horseback, transformations and more.
[*]Independent indoor and location overrides, with weapon and animation profiles.
[*]Camera noise and projectile Repulse, with optional target-lock integration.
[*]Separate nearby NPC Magic, Melee, Archery, Shouts and Transformations noise in both views.
[*]Player Hit Shake for melee, arrows, bolts and spell/staff missile impacts, with Strength, Speed, Bounce and Texture controls.
[*]Dialogue, menu, death and ragdoll cameras.
[*]Replacement Vanity Camera that stays active while you look around.
[*]Flee Framing that holds fast approaches through turns and eases back as momentum settles. New movement shouts start fresh.
[*]Horseback Drawing/Zoomed profiles and stabilized mounted target tracking.
[*]Ritual staff entries for mod-added spells across all five schools, including sneaking and target lock.
[*]Separate indoor/outdoor collision options for ground, doors, trees and walls.
[*]Preset saving, sharing, whole-tab copy/paste and controller navigation.
[*]Click slider values to type exact numbers, with immediate yellow selection.
[*]Per-entry Target Lock Pitch Bias adjusts upward/downward pitch as enemies approach.
[/list]

[b]DIET DR CAMERA 1.1.0 - REQUIREMENTS[/b]

Windows x64, Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition.
One DLL targets the known SE/AE executable versions listed in RUNTIME-SUPPORT.md,
including 1.5.97, Steam/GOG 1.6 and Steam 1.7 through 1.7.104. These are implemented
targets; full gameplay testing across every runtime has not been completed.
Skyrim VR, Microsoft Store/Game Pass and Epic builds are not supported.

[b]INSTALLATION[/b]

Install Diet Dr Camera-1.1.0.zip with MO2 or Vortex, enable it, and launch via SKSE.
Open SKSE Menu Framework with its configured shortcut (upstream default F1),
then select Diet Dr Camera. Save your tuning to a named preset in the Presets panel.
The main archive contains only the DLL and the empty stagger-camera animation
required by the mod. It does not overwrite personal presets.

SkyUI is not required by DDC. There is no ESP, MCM, or Papyrus script component.
CommonLibSSE-NG and the C++ libraries are compiled in; players do not install them.
The Source ZIP is for developers and license compliance, not installation in MO2.
Use one third-person camera overhaul; SmoothCam and DDC overlap.
Use one camera-noise implementation; Camera Noise SE and DDC overlap.

The installation/compatibility guide, credits and license files are in the
Source ZIP, available on the same mod page.

[b]Compatibility and release status[/b]
Use one third-person camera overhaul and one camera-noise implementation.
SmoothCam and Camera Noise SE overlap with DDC. Other dialogue, death-camera and
unpaused-menu mods may also overlap. Improved Camera has specific FOV integration,
but every combination has not been verified.

Gameplay testing is on Skyrim 1.6.1170, including accepted Mysticism projectile
tracing, staff Ritual selection, Vanity and first-person Hit Shake. The other
known SE/AE versions listed in RUNTIME-SUPPORT.md have incomplete gameplay coverage.
An earlier intermittent movement-stall report around werewolf combat, target lock
and menus has not yet been confirmed resolved. See RELEASE-NOTES.md.

Hit Shake starts at zero Strength. It uses native hit events and does not require
Precision. Projectile Hit Shake applies to direct actor hits and becomes softer
with distance; scenery hits, beams and lingering damage do not trigger it.

[b]Presets and updates[/b]
Save your tuning to a named preset in the Presets panel. The main download contains
no personal preset files. Public preset compatibility begins with 1.0; later updates
must preserve existing tuning and defaults. See PRESET-AUTHORS.md before sharing
presets on Nexus. Keep a backup of your personal presets when updating.
