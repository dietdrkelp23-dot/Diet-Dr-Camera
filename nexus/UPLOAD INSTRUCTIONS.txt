2. MAIN FILE

File: OmniCam-1.3.0.zip
Display name: OmniCam
Version: 1.3.0
Category: Main files
Description: Renames the mod to OmniCam and adds Combat FOV, optional DBVO 2
camera switching, and per-slider Apply across tabs or matching Indoor/Outdoor
profiles, including existing weapon and animation entries. Includes live Combat
FOV editing, death/ragdoll camera disable toggles that retain camera control and
optional slow motion, cleaner popups and improved controller navigation. Fixes
persistent sunlight-triggered shaking, rapid Whirlwind Sprint noise jerks,
cosmetic blood noise and startup issues.
Existing presets keep their values. Requires matching SKSE64, Address Library
and SKSE Menu Framework 3. DBVO 2 is optional.
Enable mod-manager downloads. Upload the ZIP itself, not its containing folder.
