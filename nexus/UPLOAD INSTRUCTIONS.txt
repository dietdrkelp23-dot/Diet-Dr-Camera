Reply to Dominic in the existing Nexus support conversation using the draft
and links in SECURITY-REVIEW.txt. Include the mod page link above.

available alongside 1.1.1. Uploading a new version does not itself complete
the staff review or guarantee a change to the security badge.

fixtures as extra main files. The two prepared ZIPs contain the intended payloads.
