DIET DR CAMERA 1.2.0 - STARTUP SUPPORT

If the game fails to start, reproduce it once and attach:
- DietDrCamera.log and skse64.log from that same launch.
- The full error message, not just its source filename and line number.
- A Crash Logger report if the game crashed and one was generated.

Tell us whether it happened before the main menu, while loading a save, or
during play, and what you were doing. Startup diagnostics are automatic;
you do not need to enable Verbose Logging.

Log folders (use your Windows Documents folder, including OneDrive redirection):
Steam: Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE\
GOG: Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition GOG\SKSE\

DDC retains three older sessions as DietDrCamera.1.log, .2.log and .3.log.
Copy the matching files soon after the failure. If normal logging is unavailable,
DDC tries %TEMP%\DietDrCamera\DietDrCamera.log. Hook errors report the log path.
If SKSE fails before loading DDC, a new DDC log cannot be created; send the
SKSE log and loader error instead.

Logs remain on your computer and are not uploaded automatically. They include
local paths and loaded module names. Send them with your support report.

The logging is intended to help diagnose remaining runtime/mod conflicts.
It is not a claim that every Skyrim version now works. See SUPPORT-LOGGING.md
and RUNTIME-SUPPORT.md in the matching source ZIP for details.
